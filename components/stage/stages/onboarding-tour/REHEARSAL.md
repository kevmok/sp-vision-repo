# onboarding-tour rehearsal

- Gold copied: `hero-demo`, read from source rather than filmed, because the skill's preview has no dependencies installed here. Taken from it: named targets, a popup placed from measured rects, and a press that changes the page under it. The `annotations` reference settled "no separate leader": the caret and the card are one object.
- Changed: Operator home at Northline Vending, day 9, with the real Pilot setup card and its four steps. The tour card is new design, since the POC has no tour. It drops under step 2, the press on Map all 6 runs the meter from 81% to 95%, the step ticks, the count rolls 1 of 4 → 2 of 4, and the card glides to step 3. The card is placed by `translate` from the measured mark, so the move is one motion. Only its words change on the way. At phone width the columns become a stepper of circles and the card carries the step's name and meter, with its caret sliding to the next circle.
- Invented for the tour: "6 products left to map", "Map all 6", "Add what you paid per case", "Skip", "Not entered". The rest of the card's strings come from `SetupChecklist` and Northline's mock data.
- Filmed: the poster at 1280 and 390, contact sheets at 700ms at both widths, and the press through the glide at 180ms at 1280. One frame in an early 1280 sheet showed the page's CSS half-applied, with every mark ticked and the card off to the left. It came while other stages were being edited on the same dev server and did not recur in the later sheets, so I read it as a hot reload.
- Known film lines: `uneven padding` on the Alerts panel, whose head is the same 44px head as Pilot setup. `touching` on the meter's count, which has a real word space. `tight control` on the phone's button against a filler row that the card covers. `overlapping` between the phone's "Sales mapped" and its meter, which looks clean in the frames. `vanishes with no exit` inside the kit's Swap, a crossfade in the frames. `kit override` on `.ss-phone*`, from Next's bundling.
- Not checked: the dark theme (the app has none), a fresh-eye review, the 60ms step-through of the glide.
